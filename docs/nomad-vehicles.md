# Nomad Vehicles

The Player Hub shows a brown Nomad · Vehicles section for characters with a ranked Nomad/Moto role.

Three slots appear initially. Filling all three reveals a second row of three, for a maximum of six. The second row stays visible while it contains any linked vehicle. Up to six existing character/mook Actors can be linked as vehicles. Players can select and see only Actors they have Observer or higher permission to view; GMs see all eligible Actors. Assigned player characters, the Nomad itself, Exec teammates, and duplicate roster links are excluded. Portraits and HP come from each linked Actor; clicking the portrait/name opens its native sheet. Links and removal history live in the Nomad's readable Nomad Vehicles Journal page. Removing a link never deletes the Actor.

Spend Downtime contains exactly one shared **Respec Nomad Vehicle** bar in its own brown Nomad section, which starts collapsed. The vehicle roster stays in the Player Hub. The bar stays visible for Nomads. Without an HQ improvement named **Garage** (case-insensitive), its actions are disabled and it displays "must have garage to use". Spend one day or all remaining days toward seven. Both progress and day spending are stored atomically in Downtime Log, survive sessions and reloads, and respect reserved Medtech days. Seven days marks completion; make vehicle changes manually. Start New Respec clears the completed bar without charging or refunding days. Removing Garage disables spending/resetting while keeping the bar and progress visible.

No vehicle HP, inventory, upgrades, or permissions are changed by this feature.
